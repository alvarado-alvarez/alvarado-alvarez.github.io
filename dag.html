Derived Algebraic
Geometry Seminar

This is a learning seminar on derived algebraic geometry coorganized by Ethan Bottomley-Mason, Peter Moody and myself.
This is a learning seminar on Derived Algebraic Geometry. Our hope is for us to first get familiar with the language of Infinity Categories and then use it to develop the modern theory of Derived Algebraic Geometry. Towards the later part of the semester our goal will be to give applications based on the participants interests (e.g. p-adic Hodge Theory, Enumerative Geometry, etc.).

    1. Infinity Categories: Introduction

    Carlos Andrés Alvarado Álvarez ??/??/26

    We give an introduction to the theory of infinity categories modeled by Joyal's quasicategories. We will introduce many concepts such as homotopy categories, homotopy (co)limits and talk about different types of fibrations and other tools that often show up.

    2. Infinity Categories: Big Ideas

    Carlos Andrés Alvarado Álvarez ??/??/26

    We introduce but don't prove a lot of important theorems and definitions. In particular, we hope to cover the Straigthening-Unstraightening equivalence, Yoneda Lemma, the Adjoint Functor Theorem for presentable infinity categories, the tensor product on presentable infinity categories and also we hope to talk about infinity operads and monoidal structures in general.

    3. Infinity Categories: Presentability

    Carlos Andrés Alvarado Álvarez ??/??/26

    We define presentable infinity categories and state the Adjoint Functor Theorem. We then define the infinity category of spectra and give the definition of a stable infinity category. Throughout we will talk about compact objects and Ind-completions and other constructions that will show up in the future.
